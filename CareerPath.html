The Problem

Job seekers aren't short on effort

They're drowning in confusion

01

Endless, conflicting advice

Searching online turns up thousands of YouTube videos and articles, each claiming a different "right way" to learn — leaving students more confused than when they started.

02

No clear learning order

There's rarely any indication of what to learn first, second, or third — learners waste time on topics before they're ready, or skip foundational basics entirely.

03

No sense of timeline

Without any idea of how long a skill actually takes, students can't plan their effort, set realistic goals, or know if they're behind or on track.

04

No way to measure progress

There's no clear signal that you're actually improving, which quietly drains motivation and causes many learners to give up halfway through.

The result: this scattered approach quietly wastes months — sometimes years — of a student's most valuable time.

How It Works

Five steps from confused to job-ready

01

Pick Your Dream Job

Tell the app the role you're aiming for — "Web Developer," "Data Analyst," or any career you can imagine.

02

Get Your Roadmap

The app instantly builds a visual, game-like chart laying out every skill you need, arranged in the right order.

03

Learn One Step at a Time

Each step arrives with the single best free video or article attached, so you never waste time searching.

04

Track Your Progress

Like leveling up in a game, you unlock the next step only after finishing the one before it.

05

Become Job-Ready

Once your roadmap is complete, the app shows real jobs and internships that match your new skills.

Features

What makes it fun & interesting

Thirteen features, organized into four pillars

Visual progress map

Your learning path is displayed as a game map, and every completed skill unlocks the next one — turning studying into a journey instead of an overwhelming list.

Points & badges

Earn points and badges as you progress, much like Duolingo — turning "getting better" into something visible and rewarding.

Daily micro-tasks

Small, bite-sized daily tasks build a habit, giving users a real reason to open the app every single day.

Friendly competition

Compare progress with friends, adding a social, competitive spark that keeps people going instead of quitting halfway.

Instant roadmap for any job

Type literally any job title and the app builds a complete, structured roadmap right away — no manual research required.

Resume-based progress check

Upload your resume and the app estimates how much of the roadmap you've already completed — never start from zero.

Outdated-skill warnings

The app flags when you're learning something becoming outdated, keeping every hour you invest genuinely relevant.

Real jobs & internships

Once a roadmap is finished, the app surfaces actual job and internship listings matching your exact skills.

Mentorship from seniors

Connect directly with people who've completed the same roadmap, getting first-hand advice and encouragement.

Salary visibility

As each step is completed, see the expected salary tied to that growing skill set — keeping the end goal tangible.

Auto-built resume & portfolio

As steps are completed, the app automatically compiles a resume and portfolio — saving you from building one from scratch.

Interview practice

Every completed roadmap ends with practice interview questions tailored specifically to that role.

Works on low internet

Designed to run smoothly even on low internet connections, keeping it accessible to students in smaller towns.
